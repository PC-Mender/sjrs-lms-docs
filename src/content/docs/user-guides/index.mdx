These guides cover everything you can do as a signed-in user: registration, profile settings, catalog browsing, borrowing flow, reservations, wishlists, and rewards.

<Aside type="note" title="Looking for staff/admin documentation?">
Operation, management, moderation, and configuration procedures are documented separately in the **Admin Guides** area (sidebar menu).
</Aside>

## Registration & Onboarding

- **[Registration Guide](./registration-guide)** — Complete guide to account creation, email confirmation, profile completion, and pending approval
- **[Registration Flow](./registration-flow)** — Plain-language, step-by-step walkthrough of each stage from the Register page through Pending Approval to dashboard activation
- **[Profile Guide](./profile-guide)** — View and manage your profile, complete profile setup, switch roles
- **[Settings Guide](./settings-guide)** — Account settings, notification preferences, and links to the dedicated security (password, MFA, sessions) pages

## Books & Catalog

- **[Catalog Guide](./catalog-guide)** — Browse, search, and filter the book catalog; reserve and wishlist from catalog (route: `/dashboard/catalog`)
- **[Loans Guide](./loans-guide)** — Borrow, return, renew, cancel, and request extensions on your own book loans (route: `/dashboard/loans`)
- **[Reservations Guide](./reservations-guide)** — Reserve unavailable books, queue system, claim ready reservations (route: `/dashboard/reservations`)
- **[Wishlist Guide](./wishlist-guide)** — Track books you want to read; priorities and management (routes: `/dashboard/wishlist/create`, `/dashboard/wishlist/edit`, `/dashboard/wishlist/show`)
- **[Book Reviews Guide](./book-reviews-guide)** — Write, edit, and manage book reviews (route: `/dashboard/book-reviews`)
- **[Journals & Magazines Guide](./journals-guide)** — Browse journals, sections, articles, and magazines (routes: `/dashboard/journals`, `/dashboard/journal-sections`, `/dashboard/articles`, `/dashboard/magazines`)

## Orders, Penalties & Financial Forms

- **[Orders Guide](./orders-guide)** — Track borrow requests submitted from the Request Cart and their review outcomes (route: `/dashboard/orders`)
- **[Penalties Guide](./penalties-guide)** — View and pay late return fines (route: `/dashboard/penalties`)
- **[TADA Guide](./tada-guide)** — Travel Allowance / Dearness Allowance claims (route: `/dashboard/tada`)
- **[PAPM Guide](./papm-guide)** — Private Account Payment Module submissions (route: `/dashboard/papm`)

## Academic Tools

- **[Academic Tools](./academic-tools)** — Overview of the Citation Generator and Plagiarism Checker modules (access depends on role and licensing)
- **[Citation Generator Guide](./citation-generator-guide)** — Generate APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE citations (route: `/dashboard/citation-generator`)
- **[Plagiarism Checker Guide](./plagiarism-checker-guide)** — Check documents for originality and matched sources (route: `/dashboard/plagiarism-checker`)

## Digital Resources

- **[Digital Resources Guide](./digital-resources-guide)** — Browse, read, and download e-books, PDFs, and online materials (routes: `/dashboard/digital-resources`, `/dashboard/digital-resources/show`, `/dashboard/digital-resources/read`)

## Personal Tools

## Notifications & Badges
