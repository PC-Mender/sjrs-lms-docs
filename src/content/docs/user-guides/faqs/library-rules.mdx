Short answers to common rule questions are below. The complete, authoritative library rules are maintained in **[Library Rules & Regulations](../../policies/library-rules-regulations)**. Always check the main regulations page for full policy text.

## Operating Hours

**Q: What are the library hours?**

Standard hours are listed in **[Library Rules & Regulations](../../policies/library-rules-regulations#operating-hours)**. Special user groups may arrange after-hours access with library administration in advance.

## Food, Phones, and Conduct

**Q: Can I bring food and drinks into the library?**

Covered drinks are generally allowed; food is permitted only in designated areas. See the full rules for details: **[Food and Drinks](../../policies/library-rules-regulations#food-and-drinks)**.

**Q: Can I take phone calls in the library?**

Keep conversations brief and quiet, set phones to silent or vibrate, and step outside for longer calls. See **[Mobile Phone Use](../../policies/library-rules-regulations#mobile-phone-use)**.

**Q: Where can I study with a group? Where are the quiet zones?**

Quiet study zones are clearly marked and reserved for silent individual work. Group study areas allow discussion at a reasonable volume. See **[Group Study and Quiet Zones](../../policies/library-rules-regulations#group-study-and-quiet-zones)**.

## Services, Printing, and Computers

**Q: Can I print or photocopy in the library?**

Services are available; per-page charges may apply, and you must comply with copyright law. See **[Printing, Photocopying, and Copyright](../../policies/library-rules-regulations#printing-photocopying-and-copyright)**.

**Q: Can I use library computers for personal work?**

Library computers are provided for academic and research use only. Session limits apply during peak hours. See **[Use of Library Computers and Electronic Resources](../../policies/library-rules-regulations#use-of-library-computers-and-electronic-resources)**.

## Conduct, Appeals, and Complaints

Questions about found items, appeals of fines or decisions, academic integrity, accessibility, or how to file a complaint are all covered in the regulations page. See the table of contents there for each topic.
