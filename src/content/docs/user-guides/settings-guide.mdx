## Overview

The Settings page is your central hub for personalizing your library experience. It is organized into five tabs: General, Academic, Display, Data Tables, and Accessibility. Security options (password change, MFA, sessions) and notification preferences are managed on dedicated pages linked from the General tab.

**Route**: `/dashboard/settings`

## Accessing Settings

1. Click your **profile avatar** in the top-right corner
2. Select **Settings** from the dropdown menu
3. Or navigate directly to `/dashboard/settings`

## Settings Tabs

### General

The General tab covers basic appearance preferences and links to security and notification configuration pages.

**Theme Mode**
- Choose between **Light**, **Dark**, or **System Default** (matches your operating system setting)
- Changes apply immediately to the interface

**Language**
- Select your preferred interface language
- Options: English, Spanish, French

**Linked Pages**
- **Notification Preferences** → opens `/dashboard/notification-preferences`
- **Security & MFA Settings** → opens `/dashboard/mfa-settings`

### Academic

Personalize how academic data is displayed and defaults are applied.

<Aside type="note">
Available options depend on your role. Students see additional defaults for their program year if configured by the institution.
</Aside>

Common Academic settings include:
- Preferred academic year default for filters
- Default semester selection for reports and catalog views
- Department/stream filtering defaults

### Display

Tune visual preferences for the application layout.

- **Density** — choose between Comfortable, Default, or Compact row spacing
- **Animation level** — reduce motion effects if preferred
- **Sidebar behavior** — pin the navigation sidebar open or let it collapse automatically
- **Font size scaling** — scale the base font size up or down

### Data Tables

Customize how lists and data tables appear throughout the system.

- **Default page size** — set how many rows appear per page (10, 20, 50, or 100)
- **Column density** — tight or relaxed spacing in tables
- **Sticky headers** — keep table headers visible while scrolling
- **Row hover highlighting** — enable or disable hover effects
- **Quick actions column** — show or hide the inline actions button column

### Accessibility

Configure interface options to suit your needs.

- **Reduced motion** — disables non-essential animations and transitions
- **High contrast** — applies enhanced contrast mode
- **Focus ring style** — thicken or color-adjust keyboard focus indicators
- **Screen reader hints** — expose additional ARIA labels for assistive technology
- **Font size boost** — small, medium, or large text scaling

## Notification Preferences

**Route**: `/dashboard/notification-preferences`

Reached via the General tab link or directly from the profile avatar dropdown.

1. Toggle which notification types you want to receive:
   - Due date reminders
   - Overdue alerts
   - Reservation-ready notifications
   - Badge awards
   - Penalty notifications
   - Announcements
2. Choose delivery channels for each type:
   - **In-app** — notifications appear in the notification center
   - **Email** — notifications sent to your registered email
3. Click **Save** to update your preferences

## Security & MFA Settings

**Route**: `/dashboard/mfa-settings`

Reached via the General tab link or directly from the profile avatar dropdown under "Security."

### Change Password

1. Enter your **current password**
2. Enter your **new password** (8–128 characters, must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character)
3. Confirm the new password
4. Click **Change Password**

### Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

1. Go to the **Two-Factor Authentication** section
2. Click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**
3. Choose your method:
   - **Authenticator App** — scan QR code with Google Authenticator or Authy
   - **Email** — receive OTP codes via email
   - **Passkey** — register your device (FaceID, TouchID) for passwordless login
4. Save your **backup codes** in a safe place
5. Use a backup code if you lose access to your primary MFA method

<Aside type="danger" title="Backup Codes">
Store your backup codes securely. If you lose access to your MFA device and don't have backup codes, you'll need to contact library administration to reset MFA.
</Aside>

### Session Management

1. Scroll to the **Active Sessions** section
2. View all devices currently logged into your account
3. See device name, approximate location, and last active time
4. Click **Revoke** to end any session you do not recognize
