## Overview

Book reviews let users share their opinions and ratings about books they have read. Reviews you write are visible across the catalog and help other patrons choose what to read next.

**Route**: `/dashboard/book-reviews`

## Viewing Reviews

### As a Regular User

1. Navigate to **Dashboard** → **Book Reviews** (`/dashboard/book-reviews`)
2. You'll see a table of your reviews with book title, rating, and status
3. The user column is hidden since you only see your own reviews

## Creating a Review

1. Navigate to the **Catalog** and open the detail page for the book you want to review
2. Find the **Reviews** section or tab on the book detail page
3. Click **Write a Review** or **+ Add Review** (button wording may vary by version)
4. Choose a **star rating** from 1 to 5 stars by clicking the star icons
5. Enter a **review title** (short summary of your opinion)
6. Write your **review body** text — share your thoughts, opinions, and recommendations about the book
7. Click **Submit**

After submitting, your review may enter a moderation queue and will need to be approved by a librarian or admin before it becomes publicly visible. You can check the status of your review from the **My Reviews** page.

## Star Rating System

The system uses a standard 5-star rating scale:

- **1 star** = Poor
- **2 stars** = Fair
- **3 stars** = Good
- **4 stars** = Very Good
- **5 stars** = Excellent

## Editing or Deleting Your Review

1. Navigate to **My Reviews** via `/dashboard/book-reviews` or from your profile page
2. Find the review you want to modify in the list
3. Click the **Edit** action to update your rating, title, or body text
4. Or click the **Delete** action to permanently remove your review

Note: If your review has already been approved by a moderator, editing it may require resubmission for re-approval before the changes become visible publicly.

## Where Reviews Appear

Once approved, reviews appear on the catalog detail page for their respective book. Individual reviews are listed below the book information, along with the reviewer's name (if profile privacy settings allow) and date. All reviews are also aggregated into an **average rating** that is displayed prominently on the book detail page and in catalog search results, giving other users a quick summary of overall reader opinion.
