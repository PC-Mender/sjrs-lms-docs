## Overview

This guide explains how to use the Citation Generator and Plagiarism Checker available under the **Academic Tools** menu. Access to these tools is determined by your account role and institution configuration. If you do not see these menu items, contact library administration to request access.

## Accessing the Tools

Both tools are available from the main dashboard navigation:

- **Citation Generator** → `/dashboard/citation-generator`
- **Plagiarism Checker** → `/dashboard/plagiarism-checker`

Menu items appear automatically when your account has the required access.

---

## Citation Generator

### Features

- **Multiple Citation Styles** — APA (7th edition), MLA (9th edition), Chicago (17th edition), Harvard, and IEEE
- **Various Source Types** — Book, Journal Article, Website, Newspaper, Film, and more
- **Citation History** — view and manage previously generated citations saved to your account
- **Copy & Export** — copy formatted citations to clipboard with one click

### Steps to Generate a Citation

1. Open **Citation Generator** from the Academic Tools menu
2. Select your preferred **Citation Style** (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE)
3. Select the **Source Type** (Book, Journal, Website, etc.)
4. Fill in the fields shown — required fields depend on the source type you selected
   - Tip: You can also search the library catalog to auto-fill book metadata instead of typing manually
5. Click **Generate Citation**
6. The formatted citation appears with the in-text citation variant beneath it
7. Click **Copy** to place it on your clipboard, or **Save Citation** to store it in your history

### Tips

- Double-check generated citations against your institution's style guide — institutional requirements sometimes differ from the default style rules
- For journal articles, include the DOI whenever available for the most accurate citation
- Saved citations are stored in your account and can be accessed from any device

### Troubleshooting

- If a required field is missing, a message appears explaining what is needed
- Ensure your account has permission to use the Citation Generator; if the menu item is not visible, contact library administration

## Plagiarism Checker

### Features

- **Originality Analysis** — heuristic-based checking of submitted text for similarity to other sources
- **Sensitivity Levels** — Low, Standard, High, and Strict modes control how aggressively matches are flagged
- **Detailed Report** — overall similarity percentage, matched passages, and list of potential sources

### Steps to Check Your Work

1. Open **Plagiarism Checker** from the Academic Tools menu
2. Either paste your text directly into the text area, or upload a supported document
   - Minimum length: 50 characters
   - Maximum length: 50,000 characters per submission
3. Provide an optional **Title** for the submission
4. Choose a **Sensitivity** level:
   - **Low** — catches only the clearest matches
   - **Standard** — recommended default for most assignments
   - **High** — flags more potential passages for your review
   - **Strict** — most sensitive, highest number of flags
5. Click **Check for Plagiarism**

### Understanding the Results

The result page shows:

- **Overall Originality Score** — percentage of content considered unique
- **Risk Level** — Low / Medium / High / Critical, based on the similarity percentage
- **Matched Passages** — highlighted sections of your text that resemble other sources, with links to the potential source

<Aside type="caution" title="Results Are Advisory">
Matches do not automatically mean plagiarism. Common phrases, bibliographic entries, and properly quoted material will sometimes register as matches. Always review the matched snippets to determine whether proper citation is needed.
</Aside>

### Tips

- Remove bibliography and reference sections before checking — they will always register as matches
- Place direct quotations in quotation marks
- Paraphrase rather than copying directly, and always cite the source
- Pair with the Citation Generator to properly credit your sources

### Troubleshooting

- Ensure your account has permission to use the Plagiarism Checker
- Very long texts may take longer to process; split into smaller sections if needed
- If a submission fails, try again with a stable internet connection
