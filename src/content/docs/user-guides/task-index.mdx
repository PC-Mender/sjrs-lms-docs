## Account & Security

### How do I reset my password?
If you have forgotten your password or need to change it:
1. Go to the **Login Page**.
2. Click the **"Forgot Password?"** link.
3. Enter your registered **email address**.
4. Check your email for a **password reset link**.
5. Click the link and enter your new password.
    *   *Note: Passwords must be 8–128 characters and include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character (e.g. `Library2024!`).*

### How do I troubleshoot login issues?
If you cannot log in:
1. **Check Credentials**: Ensure your email and password are correct.
2. **Session Limit**: You may have too many active sessions. Try logging out from other devices first.
3. **Account Lockout**: After repeated failed attempts, your account may be temporarily locked. Wait and try again, or follow the password-reset link above.
4. **Email Verification**: If your account is new, check your inbox for the email verification link — accounts must be verified before first login.
5. **Pending Approval**: Some account types require review before activation. You'll see a status page after verifying your email.
6. **Browser Issues**: Clear your browser cache or try a different browser.
7. **Contact Support**: If the issue persists, contact library help desk staff.

### How do I view my login history and active sessions?
1. Open your **Profile Menu** (top right, click your avatar).
2. Select **"Session Details"**.
3. Review your **Session History** for device, approximate location, and start/end times.
4. To end any session immediately, click **"Revoke"** next to it.
    *   *See [Settings Guide](/user-guides/settings-guide) for full details on session management.*

### How do I secure my account with two-factor authentication (2FA)?
1. Open your **Profile Menu** (top right).
2. Select **"Two-Factor Authentication"** — or navigate directly to `/dashboard/mfa-settings`.
3. Click **"Enable Two-Factor Authentication"**.
4. Choose your preferred method:
    *   **Authenticator App**: Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, Authy, or a compatible TOTP app.
    *   **Email OTP**: Receive one-time codes via your registered email address.
    *   **Passkey**: Register your device (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a hardware security key) for passwordless login.
5. Save your **Backup Codes** in a safe place — use one if you ever lose access to your primary method.
    *   *See [Settings Guide](/user-guides/settings-guide) for full details.*

## Account & Profile

### How do I switch between my roles?
If you hold multiple roles:
1. Click your **Profile Avatar** in the top right.
2. Select **"Switch Role"**.
3. Choose the role you want to use. Your dashboard and available menus update automatically.

### How do I change my notification preferences?
1. Open your **Profile Menu** → **Settings** (`/dashboard/settings`).
2. On the **General** tab, click the **"Notification Preferences"** shortcut — or go directly to `/dashboard/notification-preferences`.
3. Toggle the switches for each type of alert (due-date reminders, overdue notices, reservation-ready updates, badge awards, penalties, announcements, etc.).
4. Choose your delivery channels for each alert type: **In-App**, **Email**, or both.
5. Click **"Save Preferences"**.
    *   *See [Settings Guide](/user-guides/settings-guide) and [Notifications Guide](/user-guides/notifications-guide) for full details.*

### How do I update my profile information?
1. Open your **Profile Menu** → **"My Profile"** (`/dashboard/profile`).
2. Click **Edit Profile**.
3. Update the editable fields displayed (name, phone, stream, affiliation, etc.).
4. Click **Save**.
    *   *Note: Email address, role, and any locked student identifier fields are managed by library staff. Contact the help desk if those need updating.*
    *   *See [Profile Guide](/user-guides/profile-guide) for full details.*

### How do I view and pay a fine?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Penalties** (`/dashboard/penalties`).
2. Find the penalty with a "Pending" status.
3. Click **Pay**.
4. Complete the payment using the **UPI** flow shown on screen.
    *   *See [Penalties Guide](/user-guides/penalties-guide) for full details.*

## My Library

### How do I find and request a physical book?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Catalog** (`/dashboard/catalog`).
2. Use the search bar or filters (category, author, availability, etc.) to find the book you want.
3. Click the book card to open its details page.
4. If the book is available, click **Add to Request Cart**.
5. Open the **Request Cart** and click **Submit** to send your request.
6. Track progress in **Dashboard** → **Orders** (`/dashboard/orders`).
7. Once the request is approved and processed, the loan appears in **Dashboard** → **Loans** (`/dashboard/loans`) and the book is ready for pickup or use.
    *   *See [Catalog Guide](/user-guides/catalog-guide), [Orders Guide](/user-guides/orders-guide), and [Loans Guide](/user-guides/loans-guide) for full details.*

### How do I reserve a book that's currently checked out?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Catalog** (`/dashboard/catalog`).
2. Find the book (you can filter by Availability: "Unavailable / On Loan").
3. Click the book card, then click **Reserve This Book**.
4. Alternatively, go to **Dashboard** → **Reservations** (`/dashboard/reservations`), click **Create Reservation**, and select the book.
5. You'll join the wait queue — check the **Position in Queue** column to see when it's likely to become your turn.
    *   *See [Reservations Guide](/user-guides/reservations-guide) for full details.*

### How do I read a digital book or resource?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Digital Resources** (`/dashboard/digital-resources`).
2. Click any resource card to open its detail page.
3. Click **Read Now** to launch the built-in reader.
4. Your reading progress is saved automatically — you can resume from where you left off on any device.
    *   *See [Digital Resources Guide](/user-guides/digital-resources-guide) for full details.*

### How do I save books I'm interested in to my Wishlist?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Wishlist** (`/dashboard/wishlist`).
2. Click **Create Wishlist Item**, search for and select the book, add any personal notes, and save.
3. Or from the **Catalog** detail page, use the **Add to Wishlist** action.
4. From your wishlist you can quickly move items to the Request Cart, edit notes, or remove them.
    *   *See [Wishlist Guide](/user-guides/wishlist-guide) for full details.*

## Academic Tools & Community

### How do I generate a citation?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Citation Generator** (`/dashboard/citation-generator`).
2. Select the **source type** (book, journal article, website, chapter, etc.).
3. Search for the resource by title/ISBN/DOI, or enter the details manually.
4. Choose the **citation style** (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE, and others are available).
5. Click **Copy** to copy the formatted citation, or export the full bibliography.
    *   *See [Citation Generator Guide](/user-guides/citation-generator-guide) and [Academic Tools](/user-guides/academic-tools) for full details.*

### How do I check a document for plagiarism?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Plagiarism Checker** (`/dashboard/plagiarism-checker`).
2. Paste text directly or upload a file (.txt, .docx, .pdf).
3. Click **Check for Plagiarism**.
4. Review the **originality score**, highlighted matched passages, and the list of matched sources.
    *   *See [Plagiarism Checker Guide](/user-guides/plagiarism-checker-guide) and [Academic Tools](/user-guides/academic-tools) for full details.*

### How do I write a book review?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Book Reviews** (`/dashboard/book-reviews`).
2. Click **Create Review**.
3. Select the book, provide a **rating** (1–5 stars), and write your review text.
4. Click **Submit**. Reviews are visible to other library users immediately or after review (depending on your institution's configuration).
    *   *See [Book Reviews Guide](/user-guides/book-reviews-guide) for full details.*

### How do I manage my bookmarks?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Bookmarks** (`/dashboard/bookmarks`).
2. **Add**: Click **Create Bookmark**, enter a title and URL, apply any tags, and save.
3. **Edit**: Click the edit icon on any bookmark to update its details.
4. **Delete**: Click the trash icon and confirm. You can also bulk-select bookmarks for import or batch operations.
    *   *See [Bookmarks Guide](/user-guides/bookmarks-guide) for full details.*

### How do I keep private reading notes?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Notes** (`/dashboard/notes`).
2. Click **New Note**, give it a title, pick the related book or resource (optional), and start writing.
3. Notes auto-save as you type. Use the search bar or tag filters to find them later.
    *   *See [Notes Guide](/user-guides/notes-guide) for full details.*

### How do I see my earned badges and what's available?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **My Badges** (`/dashboard/my-badges`).
2. The **Badge Catalog** tab shows every badge in the system and its criteria.
3. The **My Badges** tab shows only the badges you have already earned.
    *   *See [Badges Guide](/user-guides/badges-guide) for full details.*

### How do I browse journal articles and magazines?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Journal Articles** (`/dashboard/articles`).
2. Use the search bar to find articles by title, author, or keyword.
3. Filter by journal, section, or publication date.
4. Click an article to view the title, authors, abstract, and full text (when access is available).
5. For journals and magazines catalog pages, use **Journals** (`/dashboard/journals`) and **Magazines** (`/dashboard/magazines`) respectively.
    *   *See [Journals & Magazines Guide](/user-guides/journals-guide) for full details.*

### How do I submit a PAPM or TADA form?
If your institution uses these modules:
- **PAPM**: Go to **Dashboard** → **PAPM Form** (`/dashboard/papm`), click **New Form**, fill in the required fields, and submit for approval. Track status from the same page.
- **TADA**: Go to **Dashboard** → **TADA Form** (`/dashboard/tada`), enter the activity/travel details, attach supporting documents, and submit.
    *   *See [PAPM Guide](/user-guides/papm-guide) and [TADA Guide](/user-guides/tada-guide) for full details.*

### How do I access Academic Reports?
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Academic Reports** (`/dashboard/academic-reports`).
2. Select the report type you need (reading progress, circulation summary, etc.).
3. Apply filters (date range, category, year level) and click **Generate Report**.
4. Download the report as CSV or PDF if required.
    *   *See [Academic Tools](/user-guides/academic-tools) for related resources.*

### How do I get help or report a problem inside the app?
Every user can submit an issue report from inside the app:
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Report an Issue** (`/dashboard/report-issue`).
2. Fill in the form:
    *   **Category** — Bug, Feature Broken, Performance, UI/UX, Data Loss, Security, or Other.
    *   **Severity** — Low, Normal, High, or Critical.
    *   **Title** — a short summary (5–200 characters).
    *   **Description** — detailed steps to reproduce the problem (10–5000 characters).
    *   **Attachments** — up to 5 screenshots (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF; up to 5 MB each).
3. Technical context (browser, page URL, app version) is captured automatically — no need to enter it manually.
4. Click **Submit**. Your report is saved and routed for review.
5. Track all your reports in the **My Reports** tab. Click **Check for Updates** on any report to pull in the latest status.
    *   *See [Issue Reports Guide](/user-guides/issue-reports-guide) for full details.*
