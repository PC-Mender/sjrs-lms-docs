## Overview

This system automatically suspends and reinstates user accounts based on two policy groups:

- **Business-rule suspension** (scheduled daily check) — triggered by overdue library materials or unpaid balances
- **Security/abuse suspension** (event-driven during sign-in) — triggered by repeated failed-access attempts

Manually applied suspensions remain in effect until a staff member reverses them; they are never lifted automatically.

## Business-Rule Suspensions (Scheduled Daily)

### When Your Account May Be Suspended

A user is automatically suspended when **either** condition below is met:

1. **Overdue threshold breach:** you have any item that is more than **30 calendar days past its due date** and has not been returned or renewed.
2. **Unpaid penalties threshold breach:** the total of unpaid fines and fees on your account reaches or exceeds **₹100**.

### When Your Account Is Automatically Reinstated

An automatically suspended account is reinstated the next business day after **both** are true:

- The suspension was originally applied by this business-rule system (not manually by staff and not by a security rule)
- Neither suspend condition still applies:
  - No items are more than 30 days overdue
  - Unpaid fines and fees total less than ₹100

### Manually Applied Suspensions Are Protected

If a librarian or administrator suspended your account manually (for any reason), the system will **not** automatically reinstate it, even if the conditions above are satisfied. Contact the library to appeal or resolve a manual suspension.

## Security / Abuse Suspensions (Event-Driven During Sign-In)

### When Your Account May Be Suspended

A user is automatically suspended for security reasons when **3 or more account lockouts occur within a rolling 24-hour window**.

A lockout is the temporary sign-in block applied after a series of failed password or verification attempts. Three consecutive lockouts on the same account within 24 hours triggers a full suspension to stop credential-guessing or abuse.

### Typical Triggers

Examples of activity that can reach the suspension threshold:

- Repeated failed sign-in attempts in a 24-hour period
- An unusually high volume of failed verification attempts
- Sign-in attempts from many different locations or devices (credential-stuffing patterns)

### Suspension Consequences

When a security suspension is applied:

- The account cannot sign in until a staff member reviews it
- All active sessions on the account are ended immediately
- The action is recorded in the account's action log

### Default Thresholds

- Lockout window: **24 hours**
- Auto-suspend after: **3 lockouts** within that window

These defaults are set by the institution and can be adjusted at the system level.

## Getting Help

If you believe your account was suspended in error, or if you have questions about a suspension, contact the library front desk or a system administrator through the help channels listed in **[Help & FAQs](../user-guides/faqs/)**.
