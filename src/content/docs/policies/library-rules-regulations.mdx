## Operating Hours

The library is open:

| Day | Hours |
|-----|-------|
| Monday – Saturday | 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Sunday | 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM |

### After-Hours Access

Library access is restricted to **operating hours**. Special user groups such as study groups or faculty may request extended access by **arranging in advance** with the library administration.

## Food and Drinks

Policies vary by library section:

- **Covered drinks:** Generally allowed in most areas.
- **Food:** Permitted only in designated areas.
- Please clean up after yourself and keep library spaces tidy. If you are unsure whether food is allowed in a specific area, check with library staff.

## Mobile Phone Use

Phone use is generally allowed, but please respect other library users:

1. Keep conversations **quiet and brief**.
2. Set phones to **silent or vibrate**.
3. **Step outside the library** for longer calls.

## Group Study and Quiet Zones

The library provides spaces for both individual quiet study and group work.

- **Quiet study zones (marked areas):** Silent, individual study only. Users must maintain silence at all times.
- **Group study areas:** Discussion is allowed at a reasonable volume. Keep noise levels appropriate and respect users in neighboring spaces.

If you are uncertain whether an area is designated quiet or group, check the posted signs or ask library staff.

## Printing, Photocopying, and Copyright

Printing and photocopying services are available within the library:

- Per-page charges may apply.
- You must **comply with all applicable copyright laws** when reproducing library materials.
- Some materials may carry additional restrictions on copying.

Check with library staff for current rates and restricted items.

## Use of Library Computers and Electronic Resources

Library computers and electronic resources are provided for **academic and research purposes only**.

Users are expected to:

1. Follow the library's acceptable use policy.
2. Respect posted session or time limits during peak hours.
3. Not engage in any activity that violates institutional policies or local, state, or national law.

## Found Items

Turn in any found item to the **library desk immediately**. The library will:

1. Record the item.
2. Hold it for a reasonable period.
3. Try to return it to the owner if it can be identified.

Do not leave found items unattended or attempt to return them personally.

## Appeals Process

If you dispute any library decision — including fines, service restrictions, or an account action:

1. **Contact the library administration** in person or via the formal help channels.
2. Provide a written or verbal summary of your concern and any supporting information.
3. The library will review the case and respond in a reasonable timeframe.

## Academic Integrity

The library expects all users to uphold academic integrity:

1. **Cite sources properly** when using library materials in assignments or publications.
2. Avoid plagiarism, fabrication, falsification, and all other forms of academic dishonesty.
3. Use the citation guides, research support, and related materials the library provides whenever you are unsure.

Serious academic integrity violations are handled jointly with the institution's academic conduct procedures.

## Accessibility Accommodations

The library provides accommodations for users with disabilities. If you need assistance or have specific access requirements:

1. **Speak with library staff** as early as possible.
2. Clearly state your specific needs.
3. The library will work with you to arrange appropriate accommodations.

Examples include accessible seating and work surfaces, large-print materials, or assistive technology.

## Complaints and Feedback

For concerns, complaints, or suggestions:

1. **Speak with library staff in person** at the service desk for immediate issues.
2. **Contact the library administration** if the issue is not resolved at the desk.
3. Submit a formal complaint through the channels provided by the institution if the matter remains unresolved.

The library values user feedback and will address concerns promptly.
