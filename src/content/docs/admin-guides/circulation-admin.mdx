## Overview

Circulation administration covers everything staff do to manage the flow of books and payments after a patron submits a request or a loan is active.

Primary routes used by this work:
- **Orders**: `/dashboard/orders` — Borrow requests (order management)
- **Loans**: `/dashboard/loans` — Active & historical loans, check-outs, returns, renewals
- **Reservations**: `/dashboard/reservations` — Queue and ready-to-claim management
- **Penalties**: `/dashboard/penalties` — Late-return penalties, waivers, and payments

## Orders Management

### Approving or Rejecting Borrow Requests

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Orders** (`/dashboard/orders`).
2. Use the **Status** filter to show only **Pending** orders.
3. Open any order to review:
   - User summary (name, role, current borrow limits, active loan count)
   - List of requested books and copies
   - Request date and any comments
4. Click **Approve** if the request complies with borrow limits and availability. Or click **Reject** and provide a reason (displayed to the user).
5. Once approved, an order moves through its workflow to generate loans.

<Aside type="tip" title="Order priority policy">
The order in which requests are taken from pending is controlled by **Order Priority Policy** at `/dashboard/order-priority-policy`. Adjust that page to change how requests are ranked when multiple users compete for copies.
</Aside>

## Loans Management

### Checking Out (Create Loan)

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Loans** (`/dashboard/loans`).
2. Click **Create Loan**.
3. Pick the **User** (search by name, email, or registration number).
4. Pick the specific **Book Copy** (copy number, location, shelf code).
5. Verify or adjust the **Due Date** (defaults to role-based borrow period).
6. Click **Create Loan**.

### Returning a Loan

1. In the **Loans** table, find the active loan.
2. Click **Return**.
3. Confirm the return. Any overdue penalty is generated automatically at this point (see Penalties Management below).

### Managing Renewals

Patrons can request renewals from their Loans page if the renewal window is open. Librarians, admins, and superusers can force-renew or cancel renewal requests:

1. Filter the Loans table for loans with pending renewal requests.
2. Open the loan, review the user's limit balance, penalties, and holds on the copy.
3. Click **Approve Renewal** to extend the due date, or **Reject Renewal** and provide a reason.

## Reservations Management

### Ready-to-Claim Workflow

When a reservation's wait position reaches 1 and a copy becomes available, the reservation becomes **Ready** and the user is notified. Staff can see the current queue from `/dashboard/reservations`.

Use the status filter to:
- **Ready** → remind user to claim or wait for automatic claim window
- **Pending** → monitor queue lengths

### Managing Expired Claims

Ready reservations that go unclaimed past their expiration date become **Expired**. The system automatically advances the next person in the queue. From the reservations page, staff can:
- Cancel pending reservations on behalf of a user
- Extend the ready window if needed (configuration-dependent)

## Penalties Management

Penalties are auto-generated on return for overdue loans. Librarians/admins can:

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Penalties** (`/dashboard/penalties`).
2. View a penalty's details: associated loan, user, amount, days overdue, payment history.
3. **Waive** a penalty (cancels it; no payment required). Provide a reason when prompted for audit.
4. **Mark as Paid** if the user paid outside the UPI flow (e.g. cash at help desk).

<Aside type="note" title="UPI payment settings">
Penalty payments use UPI configuration from **Institution Settings → UPI Payments** (`/dashboard/institution-settings?tab=upi-payments`). Enable this before patrons can pay penalties online.
</Aside>
