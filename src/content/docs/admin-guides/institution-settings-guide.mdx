## Overview

Institution Settings allow admins and superusers to configure library-wide academic parameters. These settings affect all users and operations in the system, including due-date calculations, academic reporting, and penalty payment processing.

**Route**: `/dashboard/institution-settings`

<Aside type="note" title="Access Control">
Institution Settings are restricted to **admins** and **superusers** only.
</Aside>

## Accessing Institution Settings

1. Navigate to **Dashboard** → **Institution Settings** (`/dashboard/institution-settings`)
2. The page is organized into three tabs:
   - **Academic Year** — configure the active academic year and start month
   - **Semester Dates** — define semester start/end dates used for due-date calculations and reports
   - **UPI Payments** — UPI integration for online penalty payments

Changes to each tab are saved individually. Unsaved edits on a tab are kept in draft state until you click Save on that tab.

---

## Academic Year Tab

Configure the current academic year and its annual boundaries.

### Fields

- **Current Academic Year** — the active academic year label shown throughout the system (for example, `2026-2027`)
- **Academic Year Start Month** — month when the academic year begins. Affects default date ranges in reports and analytics (for example, `June` or `July`)
- **Default Display Year** — which year is pre-selected in filters and reports for non-staff users

### Saving Changes

1. Update the fields as needed
2. Click **Save** on the Academic Year tab
3. Changes take effect immediately for all new transactions, reports, and filter defaults

---

## Semester Dates Tab

Define the start and end dates for each semester. These dates are used by:

- Due-date calculations when a borrow period crosses a semester break
- Academic reports scoped to a specific semester
- Borrow-limit enforcement windows
- Dashboard analytics and trends

### Fields

Each semester entry includes:

- **Semester Name** — displayed in filters and reports (e.g., `Semester 1`, `Monsoon`, `Spring`)
- **Start Date** — first day of the semester
- **End Date** — last day of the semester
- **Active** — toggle whether this semester is currently in use

### Managing Semesters

1. To add a new semester, fill in the empty row at the bottom and click **Save**
2. To edit an existing semester, update its fields and click **Save**
3. To remove a semester, clear its name or disable the Active toggle and click **Save**

<Aside type="caution">
Updating semester dates affects due-date and report calculations. Avoid changing historical semester dates after the semester has ended unless you are correcting a data-entry error.
</Aside>

---

## UPI Payments Tab

Configure the UPI (Unified Payments Interface) integration used when users pay penalties online.

### Fields

- **UPI ID** — your institution's UPI virtual payment address (for example, `library.college@upi`)
- **Payee Name** — the account holder name as it should appear in UPI payment apps
- **Merchant Code** — optional merchant category code, if your payment processor provides one

### Saving Changes

1. Fill in or update the payment configuration fields
2. Click **Save** on the UPI Payments tab
3. The configuration is validated and stored

<Aside type="caution" title="UPI Configuration Required for Online Payments">
Without UPI settings configured, users cannot pay penalties online. They must pay offline and have a staff member manually mark the penalty as paid.
</Aside>

### Legacy Redirect

The old route `/dashboard/upi-settings` automatically redirects to `/dashboard/institution-settings?tab=upi-payments`. No change is needed to existing bookmarks or links.

## For Superusers Only

Superusers have additional capabilities via the adjacent system administration pages:

- **Reset to defaults** — available on the Migrations page for full configuration resets during development or data recovery
- **Audit log** — view all settings changes in System Logs (filter by operation `system_settings_update`)
- **Export configuration** — download settings snapshot via the Reports or Migrations pages
